What PatchWatch is

PatchWatch SMB is an informational directory for small businesses. It lists patch deadlines, actively exploited vulnerabilities, and end of life dates. The information is general only. It is not security advice for your specific business.

What we collect on this site

There are no user accounts and no sign in on this site. The only thing the site asks for is an email address on the newsletter signup form. That address is used only to send the Weekly SMB Security Brief, and every brief includes a way to unsubscribe. Newsletter delivery is handled through Resend.

If you email us at hello@patchwatchsmb.com, we use your email only to reply to you.

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser loads those fonts, Google may receive the usual technical details a browser sends when it loads a page, such as your IP address. PatchWatch uses privacy friendly, cookieless analytics from Vercel to count page visits in aggregate, so sponsors can be shown how their listings perform. It does not use advertising trackers, does not build profiles of visitors, and does not sell data.

Sponsor payments

Sponsor payments, when available, are handled through Stripe. PatchWatch does not receive or store your card number. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to the payment details you give Stripe.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the new version on this page.
